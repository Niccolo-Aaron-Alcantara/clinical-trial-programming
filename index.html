What is clinical trial programming?

Clinical trial programming is the process of using programming languages and statisticl software to collect, clean, transform, analyze, validate, and report data gathered during clinical trials.

In simple terms, clinical trial programmers turn raw patient data into organized, reliable dataset and statistical reports that hel researchers determine whether a drug or treatment is safe and effective.
